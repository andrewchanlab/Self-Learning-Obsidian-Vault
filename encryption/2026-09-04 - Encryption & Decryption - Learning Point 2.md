# Symmetric Encryption Uses One Key for Lock and Unlock

AES (Advanced Encryption Standard) is the gold standard for symmetric encryption. The same key encrypts and decrypts. The challenge: how do you share the key securely? You can't send it over the same channel you're trying to protect — this is called the key distribution problem.

## Key Takeaway
Symmetric encryption (AES) is fast and efficient but requires secure key exchange — the fundamental challenge that asymmetric encryption solves.
