# HSM Form Factors — From USB Tokens to Network Appliances

Hardware Security Modules come in many shapes. USB tokens (like YubiKey or smart cards) are portable, low-cost HSMs for individual key storage. Rack-mounted network HSMs are high-throughput appliances serving entire data centers. Cloud HSMs (AWS CloudHSM, Azure Dedicated HSM) provide managed HSM infrastructure without physical hardware. The right form factor depends on key usage volume, throughput requirements, and whether you need physical custody.

## Key Takeaway
HSMs range from pocket-sized USB tokens to enterprise rack appliances — pick the form factor that matches your throughput needs and custody requirements.

# HSM 形態 — 從 USB 令牌到網絡設備

硬件安全模組有多種形態。USB 令牌（如 YubiKey 或智慧卡）是便攜、低成本的 HSM，適用於個人金鑰儲存。機架式網絡 HSM 是高吞吐量的設備，可為整個資料中心提供服務。雲端 HSM（AWS CloudHSM、Azure Dedicated HSM）提供託管式 HSM 基礎設施，無需管理實體硬體。選擇哪種形態取決於金鑰使用量、吞吐量需求以及是否需要實體保管。

## 重點摘要
HSM 的形態從口袋大小的 USB 令牌到企業級機架設備不等 — 選擇符合您吞吐量需求和保管要求的形態。
