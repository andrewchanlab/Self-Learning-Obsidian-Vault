# HSMs Are Tamper-Resistant Cryptographic Vaults

A Hardware Security Module (HSM) is a dedicated hardware appliance designed to protect cryptographic keys. Keys stored in an HSM never leave the device — all cryptographic operations (signing, decryption) happen inside the tamper-resistant hardware. If someone tries to physically or logically breach the HSM, it erases its keys. Banks, payment networks, and certificate authorities use HSMs to protect their most sensitive keys.

## Key Takeaway
HSMs provide the highest level of key protection — cryptographic keys never exist outside the secure hardware boundary.
