# Meilisearch Prioritizes Developer Experience Over Features

Meilisearch is a minimalist search engine built in Rust. It runs as a single binary, starts instantly, and requires zero configuration. Elasticsearch needs clusters, JVM tuning, and 4GB RAM minimum. Meilisearch runs well under 100MB and on a Raspberry Pi.

## Key Takeaway
Meilisearch trades Elasticsearch's enterprise complexity for simplicity — a single binary, zero config, and instant setup.

## 中文：Meilisearch 将开发者体验置于功能之上

Meilisearch 是一款用 Rust 编写的极简搜索引擎。它作为单一二进制文件运行，启动迅速，零配置。Elasticsearch 需要集群、JVM 调优和至少 4GB 内存，而 Meilisearch 在 100MB 以下就能良好运行，甚至可以在树莓派上运行。

## 核心要点
Meilisearch 以简单换取 Elasticsearch 的企业级复杂性——单一二进制文件、零配置、即装即用。
