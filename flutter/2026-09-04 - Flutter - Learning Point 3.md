# State Management — The Core Challenge in Flutter

Flutter has no built-in reactive binding like Vue or Angular. You must choose how to propagate state changes across the widget tree. Options range from setState (simple), InheritedWidget (shared state), Provider (recommended for mid-sized apps), to Riverpod, Bloc, or GetX — each trading simplicity for scalability.

## Key Takeaway
State management is Flutter's most important architectural decision — choosing the right approach depends on your app's complexity and team familiarity.
