# Certificate Types — DV, OV, and EV

Domain Validation (DV) certificates verify you control the domain (email or DNS check). Organization Validation (OV) certificates verify the organization exists. Extended Validation (EV) certificates require rigorous business verification — your browser shows a green bar with the company name. Let's Encrypt provides free DV certificates, making HTTPS ubiquitous.

## Key Takeaway
Certificate validation levels range from basic domain control (DV) to rigorous business verification (EV) — each serves different trust requirements.
